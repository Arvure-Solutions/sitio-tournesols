Se former au soin, une expérience transformative pour une carrière en bien-être et thérapie

In a fast-changing world, choosing a career in care, well-being and therapy is more than a professional option: it is a vocation that transforms lives. Training to support others brings significant personal and professional growth.

Les bénéfices de se former au soin

Learning well-being techniques builds technical skills and inner transformation: empathy, patience, compassion, resilience. Professionally, opportunities range from individual support to well-being group facilitation.

Les profils concernés

Career changers seeking values-aligned work, educators and social workers expanding their skills, and anyone drawn to mental health and well-being.

L’approche unique de l’École Les Tournesols

Integrative teaching blending theory and practice, relational skills, personal reflection, and continuing education — training autonomous, empathic professionals.
